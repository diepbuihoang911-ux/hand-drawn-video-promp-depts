# Flow 图生视频提示词｜AI CapEx 原文案 01–07

## 使用方式

- 模式：`Video → Frames → First + last`
- 画幅：9:16
- 时长：6 秒
- 首帧：统一空白暖白纸
- 尾帧：对应编号的完成静帧
- 音频：关闭或生成后移除

统一空白首帧可使用：

```text
A completely empty vertical 9:16 sheet of clean warm-white sketchbook paper with very subtle fine paper grain and even soft lighting. No marks, no drawings, no objects, no text, no border, no shadow, no gradient and no watermark.
```

如果当前模型只能把完成静帧作为首帧，不要要求空纸开场。把对应提示词的开头替换为：

```text
Animate the supplied completed illustration in place for 6 seconds while preserving the exact composition and every drawn shape. Use only restrained paper-cutout micro-motion: one or two small object bounces, a short hinge-like hand movement, subtle wheel rotation or a single drawn accent appearing. ```
```
